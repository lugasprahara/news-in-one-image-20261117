One 1024×1024 infographic that sums up Tuesday 17 November 2026's world news in eight verified stories — from the collapsed Crimea Bridge and Iran's drone attack on Bahrain to Mongolia's resigning president, a Canada–China tariff deal and a magnitude 6.6 earthquake in Indonesia. Every story was cross-checked against at least two independent sources listed in SOURCES.md; the artwork is a hand-built HTML/SVG page (index.html) rendered to entry.png, with no external images or fonts.
