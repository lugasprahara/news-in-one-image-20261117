Version 2 of the one-image summary of Tuesday 17 November 2026: a single 1024×1024 infographic carrying 32 verified stories from six continents — 11 illustrated panels (the fallen Kerch Bridge span, Iran's drones over Bahrain, Gaza's flooded tents, Trump in Manila, Japan's scrambled jets, Mongolia's resigning president, the Xi–Carney tariff deal in Shenzhen, UK sanctions on settler builders, France's pay marches, a Banda Sea earthquake and COP31's decisive week) plus a schematic "Where it happened" map and 21 further briefs. The design is an original deep-teal, warm-paper and coral newspaper layout with hand-drawn flat SVG scenes throughout. Every story was checked against reputable reporting dated to that Tuesday — including an explicit day-check per story in SOURCES.md — and widely circulated wrong-day items (a 16 November tanker claim, a 16 November bank outage, a 15 November shooting) were deliberately left out. The artwork lives in index.html and is rendered to entry.png; no external images or fonts are used.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
