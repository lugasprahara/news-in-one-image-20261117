## 1. CRIMEA BRIDGE COLLAPSES
- As shown: "Kerch road span falls into the strait after Ukraine strike"
- Facts: Ukraine severely damaged the Kerch (Crimean) Bridge linking
  Russia to occupied Crimea in a second consecutive overnight strike.
  Images shared by Ukrainian officials show a section of the road
  bridge — an arch and span — collapsed into the Kerch Strait.
  President Zelenskyy called it an "unprecedented operation" weeks in
  the making; the Kremlin said the bridge would be rebuilt "better
  than before" and traffic was suspended. Russia had earlier hit
  Kyiv's Northern and Southern bridges over the Dnipro overnight.

## 2. IRAN DRONES TARGET BAHRAIN
- As shown: "US radar at Isa base — intercepted, no damage"
- Facts: On Tuesday 17 Nov, Iran fired drones at a US military radar
  system at the Sheikh Isa (Isa) air base in Bahrain, home to the US

## 3. JAPAN JETS SCRAMBLE
- As shown: "Two suspected Russian planes enter airspace off Okinoshima"
- Facts: Two suspected Russian aircraft entered Japanese territorial
  airspace northwest of Okinoshima Island, Fukuoka Prefecture, early
  on Tuesday 17 Nov — at about 4:07–4:10 am and 4:09–4:12 am, roughly
  three minutes each. Japan scrambled Air Self-Defense Force fighters,
  issued radio warnings and lodged a "very stern protest" with Moscow.
  First confirmed Russian violation since 23 September 2024.

## 5. MONGOLIA PRESIDENT QUITS
- As shown: "Khurelsukh resigns over $25M graft claim — denies it"
- Facts: Mongolian President Khurelsukh Ukhnaa announced on Tuesday
  17 Nov that he will resign, after the opposition Democratic Party
  accused him and top officials of graft and a former Ulaanbaatar
  mayor alleged a $25 million payment linked to the
  Tavantolgoi–Gashuunsukhait railway project. Khurelsukh denied
  wrongdoing, said he had never stolen from the state, and said he
  would cooperate with investigators. Parliament must accept the
  resignation.

## 6. CANADA–CHINA CUT TARIFFS
- As shown: "Xi–Carney deal gives relief on $280M of exports"
- Facts: Meeting in Shenzhen on Tuesday 17 Nov ahead of the APEC
  summit, Chinese President Xi Jinping and Canadian Prime Minister
  Mark Carney agreed to extend and expand tariff relief: China will
  provide additional tariff relief on about C$400 million
  (US$280 million) of Canadian exports, including frozen pork cuts
  and fish and seafood; Canada will extend remission measures for
  certain Chinese steel and aluminium products by two years.

## 8. QUAKE SHAKES INDONESIA
- As shown: "M6.6 Banda Sea, near Tual — no tsunami threat"
- Facts: A magnitude 6.6 earthquake struck the Banda Sea about
  148 km west-southwest of Tual, Indonesia, at 06:11 local time on
  Tuesday 17 Nov (21:11 UTC on 16 Nov), at 68 km depth (USGS).
  Indonesia's BMKG measured it higher (7.1, later revised) and said
  it had no tsunami potential; tremors were felt across northern
  Australia including Darwin, with no tsunami threat there. No
  casualties or major damage were reported.
- Sources:
